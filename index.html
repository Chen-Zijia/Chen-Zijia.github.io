Biography

I am Zijia Chen (陈梓佳), a Ph.D. student at Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Xiaojie Mao. Before starting my Ph.D., I received my bachelor's degrees from Tsinghua University — a Bachelor in Economics and Finance from the School of Economics and Management, and a Bachelor in Statistics from the Department of Statistics and Data Science.

Currently, I mainly work on methods related to causal inference, statistical machine learning, and data-driven decision making. If you share similar interests or would like to exchange ideas, feel free to reach out!
